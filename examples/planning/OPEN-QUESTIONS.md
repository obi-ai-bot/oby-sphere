# Open questions

## Receipts privacy review (blocks v2.1)

Do we store receipt images at all? Legal's first read: storing them makes us hold personal data (names, card digits on receipts) for as long as we keep them. Planning leans towards keeping only a hash on the server and the image on the phone. Owner: planning. Due: before v2.1 starts.

## Settle-up across currencies

When a group mixes currencies, settle-up picks the group's currency. Several testers wanted to settle in the currency they paid in. Needs a spec change; the spec team asked for usage numbers first.

## v1 shutdown date

The roadmap says v1 stays read-only for 90 days after v2 ships. Support wants 180 for users who rarely open the app. Undecided.
