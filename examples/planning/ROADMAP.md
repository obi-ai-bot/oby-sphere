# Kite roadmap

## Now: v2 API (target: end of January)

1. Groups, expenses and settle-up on the v2 API (spec: the spec team's SPEC.md).
2. Idempotency keys on every write: the most requested fix from support (duplicate expenses on flaky networks).
3. Move the iOS and Android apps to v2; v1 stays read-only for 90 days.

## Next: v2.1 (February to March)

1. Recurring expenses (rent, subscriptions).
2. Receipts as images, once the privacy review decides how they're stored.
3. Export to CSV and to accounting tools.

## Later

- Cards: a shared virtual card per group (needs a banking partner; exploratory talks only).
- Multi-currency balances shown in each member's own currency.

## Not doing

- Ads, or selling spending data. Kite is paid by subscription.
