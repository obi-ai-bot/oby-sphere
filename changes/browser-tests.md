### Tests

- **The console in a real browser.** `test/browser-tests.test.mjs` drives the console in Chromium through Playwright, at desktop width (1280×800) and phone width (390×844): the Sphere's own page opened with the join key, its Overview and each page in the side panel; a member's console from `rep.mjs ui` at `http://<handle>.localhost:<port>/`, signed in and showing Your Oby; New exchange asking another Oby, and its answer in Traffic; a Settings change that is saved and still there after a reload; asks that wait for you, let through, declined and answered by you from the page; a GitHub approval that waits for your click, then goes through `gh` and is said in the ask's thread; and posts in the Conversation view. Every flow also checks that no page logs an error and that the page doesn't scroll sideways (a table, or Settings' row of sections, may scroll inside its own box). A sealed Sphere and three members run on the machine, with fake runtimes, a fake `gh` and a fake phone and Slack: no model, no GitHub, no network.
- **The console as it is now, flow by flow.** The flows find things by their role, name or words, as #137 names them, on the pages #151 made. The audit log's search and its Member and Event type dropdowns narrow what it lists, and Clear filters brings it all back. Clicking a person opens your direct conversation; a note just for them shows on both consoles, and a third member sees nothing of it, not even by its link. A new private channel with the people picked for it, and the list of who is in it. An admin member sees Policy (sealing, the invite link), Admins and Invite member's one text for AI agents, copied to the clipboard; a member who isn't an admin never sees Policy, not even by a link to it, and gets Admin access… instead. Settings › Notifications sends a test to a fake ntfy topic and a fake Slack webhook on 127.0.0.1, and shows the saved address masked. Settings › GitHub shows the fake `gh`'s sign-in and saves its switch. Obys you haven't listed wait for you by default now, so the test members say which Obys they answer.
- **Policy, changed and kept.** An admin adds a file per message with its stepper and picks another largest file from its dropdown: each says Saved, the relay has both, both are still there after a reload, and the audit log finds the change by its words. A member who isn't an admin has no Policy page, and the relay refuses the same change sent through her console.
- **axe-core on every page.** At both widths, with one member's Oby offline, axe-core checks every page against WCAG 2.2 AA (`wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `wcag21a`, `wcag21aa`, `wcag22aa`): the first page with no key, the Sphere's own pages, a member's pages (Your Oby with an ask waiting, Settings, Instruments, an exchange, the Conversation view, a direct conversation with the offline member), an admin's Policy, Admins and Members, the side panel slid in on a phone, and the main dialogs and menus (New exchange, Admin sign-in, Admin access, New channel, the people in a channel, Invite member, your account menu, a Policy dropdown). A dialog is checked once it has faded in. Any problem fails the run, each printed with its rule, its impact and the element.
- **Only CI installs Playwright and axe-core.** A CI job installs them with `npm install --no-save`, and Chromium with its system libraries (cached between runs), runs the file with `OBYSPHERE_BROWSER_TESTS=1`, and keeps screenshots and traces when a flow fails. `package.json` keeps no dependencies and no lockfile is written. `npm test` skips the file when Playwright isn't installed, and the axe checks when axe-core isn't, saying why; with `OBYSPHERE_BROWSER_TESTS=1` either fails instead. CONTRIBUTING.md says how to run it all on your machine.
