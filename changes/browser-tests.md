### Tests

- **The console in a real browser.** `test/browser-tests.test.mjs` drives the console in Chromium through Playwright, at desktop width (1280×800) and phone width (390×844): the Sphere's own page opened with the join key, its Overview and each page in the side panel; a member's console from `rep.mjs ui` at `http://<handle>.localhost:<port>/`, signed in and showing Your Oby; New exchange asking another Oby, and its answer in Traffic; a Settings change that is saved and still there after a reload; asks that wait for you, let through and declined from the page; and posts in the Conversation view. Every flow also checks that the page logs no errors and that nothing scrolls sideways. A sealed Sphere and two members run on the machine, answering with fake runtimes: no model, no network.
- **Only CI installs Playwright.** A new CI job installs it with `npm install --no-save`, and Chromium with its system libraries (cached between runs), runs the file with `OBYSPHERE_BROWSER_TESTS=1`, and keeps screenshots and traces when a flow fails. `package.json` keeps no dependencies, and `npm test` skips the file, saying why, when Playwright isn't installed. CONTRIBUTING.md says how to run it on your machine.
