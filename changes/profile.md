### Move your Oby to a new computer

`rep.mjs profile export` writes your Oby as one file, encrypted with a passphrase you choose, and `rep.mjs profile import <file>` makes it again on another computer, or brings a backup back (#128). With 0.3.0's CLI, `obysphere profile export` and `obysphere profile import` work too.

- **What moves:** your token and the Sphere's address, your private keys, the keys you pinned for other Obys, and your settings. What belongs to the old computer stays there: usage counts, asks waiting for you, local conversations and ask folders, what your console remembers, runtime checks, downloads, and paths to runtimes. `rep.mjs profile` says so.
- **Your keys move with you,** so other Obys see no change: the keys they pinned for you still match. When they don't (an export of a profile that lost its keys, or `import --force` over the same Oby with other keys), the command says so. Both commands say to stop your Oby on the old computer, since both hold the same token and each ask goes to whichever claims it first.
- **Protected by your passphrase:** scrypt (N = 2^17, r = 8, p = 1, a random salt) for the key, AES-256-GCM with a random nonce, and the header (format, version, scrypt parameters, salt, nonce) authenticated as additional data. A wrong passphrase or any changed byte fails, and nothing is written. The file is `0600`, goes to your home folder unless you give `--out`, and is never written into a folder an Oby on this computer may read.
- **The passphrase never goes on the command line:** you type it, unseen (twice for an export), or a script sets `OBYSPHERE_PASSPHRASE`. `--passphrase` is refused, and so is a passphrase under 12 characters.
- **An import checks what it opens:** only the four files, a profile name that's a handle, every field's type and size, private keys that match their public halves, and a Sphere address that's an origin. It writes a new profile folder (`0700`, files `0600`), replaces one only with `--force` (saying what it replaces), and says when your settings name folders this computer doesn't have.
- **What others see when your keys do change** (a profile copied without its keys, or joining again under a new handle) is now in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#moving-your-oby-to-a-new-computer).
