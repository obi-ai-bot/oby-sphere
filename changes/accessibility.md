### The console

- **Keyboard paths through every page and dialog.** A "Skip to the content" link comes first. Console and Conversation are tabs: the arrow keys, Home and End move between them. The + menu and your account menu take the arrow keys, and Escape or Tab closes them and goes back to their button. Each exchange in Overview, Traffic and Your Oby has a button, so its row opens with Enter. On a phone, tabbing into the side panel slides it in, and Escape slides it away and goes back to its toggle. Settings' and Policy's own controls work the same way: a dropdown opens with the arrow keys and Escape gives the focus back to it, a stepper takes the arrow keys, and a set of choices is a radio group you move through with the arrow keys.
- **Dialogs hold the focus.** New exchange, Invite member, Admin access and New channel make the page behind them inert, keep Tab and Shift+Tab inside, close on Escape (an open menu closes first), and give the focus back to what opened them (even when the page was redrawn meanwhile; to the menu's button when an item of your account menu or the + menu opened them), or to the new page's title when they took you somewhere.
- **Focus survives a busy Sphere.** The console redraws its side panel and page as the Sphere moves. A part whose markup hasn't changed is left as it is, so a screen reader doesn't read the focused control out again every few seconds: Members, Overview's numbers, the audit log's results and Policy too. When a part does change, the control that had the focus is found again in the new markup, so the keyboard isn't sent back to the top of the page. It's found only as the same control: after Suspend, the focus is on that member's Resume, never on another member's button, and after you decide an ask it goes to the page's title, not to the next ask's buttons.
- **Names for everything.** Icon-only buttons, every field (the key on the first page, your own answer in Waiting for you, the audit log's search), the view tabs, the side panel ("Pages", "Conversations") with the current page and conversation marked, Policy's switches with their state, its steppers and dropdowns named by their row (and described by the line under it, such as "0 turns it off"), the audit log's dropdowns with what they pick, the − and + of a stepper in Settings with the number they change, unread counts ("3 new") and the Waiting for you count ("2 waiting for you"), a member's state, a private channel, and an offered instrument's owner and whether their Oby is there. A route of pictures in Traffic and Files says who asked whom in words. The Sphere's tile and your picture at the side panel's edge are named pictures, not buttons that did nothing. The Conversation view has a title, a page's sections are headings under it, and tables that scroll sideways on a phone can be scrolled with the keyboard.
- **A polite voice for live updates.** New messages, the map, the live log, what waits for you, copies, Policy's "Saved" and problems are said in one polite region. Each kind waits a moment for more of the same, then is said as one sentence ("6 new messages in #general, the last from Bob Test."); the live log at most every 15 seconds, the map every 8. The thread and the live log no longer read out every line, and the seconds that tick are never read.
- **Contrast at WCAG 2.2 AA.** Grey text is lighter (`--muted` is macOS's systemGray, `--label` half white), so every text colour reads at 4.5:1 on every surface. Field edges reach 3:1, for every field and anything you pick a value from (Settings' and Policy's dropdowns and steppers, the audit log's search and filters), and so does the focus ring, one colour everywhere. A dropdown's active option has the ring too. The unread badge is a darker blue so its white numbers read at 4.5:1. In Settings, a picked choice and a toggle that's on have an edge at 3:1, not just a faint fill; so do the people you pick for a channel. An empty conversation's words, offline members' names and an away Oby's instruments are no longer faded below 4.5:1. An away Oby's picture goes grey instead of faint, so its letters still read at 4.5:1, as they do in every colour a picture has. Links inside messages are underlined. With macOS's Increase contrast the greys and edges go brighter still; Windows' contrast themes keep switches, the chosen item, choice or option, and status dots visible. With reduced motion, no dots fly across the map.

### Tests

- **`test/accessibility.test.mjs`**, with no browser: every button the page or its script makes has a name, every field a label, and every call to the console's own controls a label or the row title it's labelled by; Settings' controls and Policy, rendered, have their roles, names, states and descriptions; the dialogs are modal, named, and hold and return the focus; the focus code, run on a small made-up DOM, finds the same control again after a redraw (never another member's or ask's) and gives the focus back to a dialog's opener; the view switch is tabs and the side panel's lists are navigation; the map, the live log, the thread and what waits for you have their regions and reach the voice; the voice, run on a made-up clock, sums up a burst in one sentence and keeps the live log to one in 15 seconds; and every text colour, field edge, focus ring and picture's letters meet WCAG 2.2 AA in each theme the page defines, worked out from its CSS tokens.
