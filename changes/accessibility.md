### The console

- **Keyboard paths through every page and dialog.** A "Skip to the content" link comes first. Console and Conversation are tabs: the arrow keys, Home and End move between them. The + menu and your account menu take the arrow keys, and Escape or Tab closes them and goes back to their button. Each exchange in Overview, Traffic and Your Oby has a button, so its row opens with Enter. On a phone, tabbing into the side panel slides it in, and Escape slides it away and goes back to its toggle.
- **Dialogs hold the focus.** New exchange, Invite member, Admin access and New channel make the page behind them inert, keep Tab and Shift+Tab inside, close on Escape (an open menu closes first), and give the focus back to what opened them (even when the page was redrawn meanwhile), or to the new page's title when they took you somewhere.
- **Focus survives a busy Sphere.** The console redraws its side panel and page as the Sphere moves. A part whose markup hasn't changed is left as it is, so a screen reader doesn't read the focused control out again every few seconds. When a part does change, the control that had the focus is found again in the new markup, so the keyboard isn't sent back to the top of the page. It's found only as the same control: after Suspend, the focus is on that member's Resume, never on another member's button, and after you decide an ask it goes to the page's title, not to the next ask's buttons.
- **Names for everything.** Icon-only buttons, every field (the key on the first page, your own answer in Waiting for you, Policy's choices), the view tabs, the side panel ("Pages", "Conversations") with the current page and conversation marked, Policy's switches with their state, the audit log's filters, unread counts ("3 new") and the Waiting for you count ("2 waiting for you"), a member's state and a private channel. The Sphere's tile and your picture at the side panel's edge are named pictures, not buttons that did nothing. The Conversation view has a title, and tables that scroll sideways on a phone can be scrolled with the keyboard.
- **A polite voice for live updates.** New messages, the map, the live log, what waits for you, copies and problems are said in one polite region. Each kind waits a moment for more of the same, then is said as one sentence ("6 new messages in #general, the last from Bob Test."); the live log at most every 15 seconds, the map every 8. The thread and the live log no longer read out every line, and the seconds that tick are never read.
- **Contrast at WCAG 2.2 AA.** Grey text is lighter (`--muted` is macOS's systemGray, `--label` half white), so every text colour reads at 4.5:1 on every surface. Field edges and the focus ring reach 3:1, and the unread badge is a darker blue so its white numbers read at 4.5:1. An empty conversation's words and offline members' names are no longer faded below 4.5:1, and links inside messages are underlined. With macOS's Increase contrast the greys and edges go brighter still; Windows' contrast themes keep switches, the chosen item and status dots visible. With reduced motion, no dots fly across the map.

### Tests

- **`test/accessibility.test.mjs`**, with no browser: every button the page or its script makes has a name, and every field a label; the dialogs are modal, named, and hold and return the focus; the focus code, run on a small made-up DOM, finds the same control again after a redraw (never another member's or ask's) and gives the focus back to a dialog's opener; the view switch is tabs and the side panel's lists are navigation; the map, the live log, the thread and what waits for you have their regions and reach the voice; the voice, run on a made-up clock, sums up a burst in one sentence and keeps the live log to one in 15 seconds; and every text colour, field edge and focus ring meets WCAG 2.2 AA in each theme the page defines, worked out from its CSS tokens.
