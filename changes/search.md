### Search the messages you can see

- **Search in the Conversation view** (#138). A field at the top of its side panel (`/` gets you there when you aren't typing). Every word must appear, case and accents don't matter, and "quoted words" match as a phrase. It matches pieces of words, so it works for languages written without spaces. `from:bob`, `in:#design`, `with:bob` (your direct conversation) and `has:file` narrow it. Results take the thread's place, newest first, each with who, where ("#design", "with Bob Test, directly"), when, and the words found marked. A click opens the conversation at that message. A line says what it covered ("Your Oby searched 4,980 messages, back to 3 November. 212 more messages here were sealed for others, or before you joined: your Oby can't open them.").
- **Your Oby searches everything the Sphere keeps that it can open.** In a sealed Sphere only your Oby can open what was sealed for you, so the search runs there. Your console (`rep.mjs ui`) keeps an index in its memory, never on disk: built when a console page first loads, by reading the history back 200 messages at a time and opening each as it does for the page; kept up from the page's live stream, a catch-up at each page load and every five minutes while no page is open; read again once a day. It holds the newest 20,000 messages. What it can't open is counted, not searched. A message whose signature doesn't verify is found, and marked as in the thread. A page of deleted messages doesn't end the read of the history.
- **What leaves the Sphere leaves your Oby's search** (#139). A deleted message leaves the index at once while a console page is open. So does one the Sphere removed because it keeps messages for a set time, or only the newest. With no page open, it leaves at the next catch-up, within five minutes: each catch-up, and the day's fresh read, also reads the Sphere's audit log on from where the last one stopped, for its `delete` and `expire` events. A message older than the Sphere keeps them (`keepDays`) is never found, and leaves the index, as the relay hides it at once. When a removal's newest message isn't in the index (it was in a conversation you're not in), your Oby asks the relay for a few single messages to find where the removal ended. Your console also forgets the keys it opened for that message's files. What the relay says can take messages out of the index, never keep one in.
- **The relay hears nothing of it.** A search, and opening a result (its conversation comes from the index), send the relay no request. It never learns your words, or whether you searched: it sees your Oby read the history, and the audit log, when a page loads and at the catch-ups. The query goes in a POST body to your own machine, never in a URL or a log.
- **As a member, for an admin too.** It covers public channels and the private channels and direct conversations your Oby is in, though the relay lets an admin read every channel.
- **The Sphere's own page searches what it holds.** Opened with the join key alone, or with a console from before search, the page searches the messages it holds itself, and says how many it couldn't open.
- **`obysphere search "<words>" [--in #design] [--from bob] [--with bob] [--has file] [--json]`** asks your running console. With no console running, or before a console page has loaded, your Oby reads the Sphere's history now to search it, and says so.
- **`sphere_search`, in your own Claude Code session.** Results come back as quoted data. It's never in the session that answers for others, so another member's ask can't search your direct messages. It asks your console, so its requests aren't tagged with its name, and the Instruments page doesn't count it.
- **Your console's local API:** `POST /local/search { q, before?, limit?, wait? }` and `POST /local/search/around { id }` (SETTINGS.md), POST only, with the console's header, from its own origin, like the rest of `/local/*`.
- **Your console ends its stream to the relay when its page goes.** Before, once a garbage collection had run, Node's fetch could miss the abort, and the stream stayed open: each reload could leave one more, until the relay's limit of 8 streams per agent refused the page's. Now it ends by the relay's next ping, within 20 seconds.

### Tests

- **`test/search.test.mjs`.** The page's matching functions, run in `vm`: every word, phrases, case and accents, Japanese, Russian and Greek, the filters, a sealed message that wasn't opened counted and not searched, the conversations an admin sees but isn't in left out, the Sphere's own page kept to public channels, and words holding HTML kept as text. rep.mjs's copies are the page's, word for word. Then a sealed Sphere on 127.0.0.1 with alice, bob and sam, a private channel, direct messages and about 2,500 messages, read in pages through a proxy that logs every request and plays a malicious relay: alice, an admin, finds her direct messages and the public posts, never bob and sam's conversation or a private channel she isn't in; posts sealed before she joined are counted; a forged signature is marked; a tombstone isn't indexed and a `message-delete` event takes a message out; a search and a click on a result send the relay nothing, and the words are in no URL; `/local/search` refuses a call without the console's header, from another origin, from another site, by GET or for another Host. `obysphere search` with the console running (no request to the relay) and without it (it reads the history, untagged, and says so); `sphere_search` fenced in your own session, missing from the answering one, and never counted in `GET /api/stats`, with the console running or not. A file sent in the open still comes through the console once the index is built. With no page open, the console's stream to the relay ends, and a catch-up reads back to where the last read stopped, though the page read the newest messages first. Then a Sphere with alice, bob, carol and dan: with a page open, a message bob deletes leaves at once; with none, one deleted more than a page of history back leaves at the next catch-up, through the audit log, and its read of the history stops at the newest page; when the operator keeps only the newest 100, the messages removed leave at once with a page open and at the next catch-up without, the removal's newest message being in a channel the reader isn't in. Relays the test plays: a message older than `keepDays` is never found, as soon as the console hears the policy, and the next catch-up hears it alone; a removal whose newest message the index holds takes out every one before it, odd audit events take out nothing else, and a removed message the relay sends again stays out. A relay that sends an endless history is cut off at 20,000 messages. A relay that sends a page of deleted messages is read past it, and a private channel or a direct conversation it lists late is asked about before anything in it is kept or left out. A console no page has loaded reads the history for the first search from a terminal, and says so.
- **In a browser.** `/` reaches the field, Enter opens the results, the words found are marked, and a click opens the message in its conversation, with no request to the relay, at desktop and phone width. On the Sphere's own page a search says what it couldn't open. axe-core checks the results.
