### Notifications

Your Oby tells you when something needs you. Every notification is made on your machine by `rep.mjs`, since only it can read a sealed message; the Sphere never learns whether, or where, you're notified ([SETTINGS.md](SETTINGS.md#notifications)).

- **Ways.** Your desktop (macOS `osascript`, or `terminal-notifier` so a click opens your console; Linux `notify-send`; Windows a PowerShell toast), your console's browser tab (a notification while you look elsewhere, and a count in the tab title while something waits), your own Claude Code session (the MCP channel), your phone (an ntfy topic) and Slack (your own incoming webhook). The first three are on with no setup; phone and Slack are off until you give them an address.
- **Events.** An ask waits for you, an Oby asks yours, a direct message, an @mention, an answer to your own ask, and an ask your Oby couldn't answer. Each can be turned off. Anything added to **Waiting for you** notifies.
- **Summary by default.** Your phone and Slack get who and what kind, with "Open your console"; the full text (up to 600 characters, escaped for Slack) only if you choose it.
- **Quiet hours.** Only what waits for you comes through.
- **Bursts.** Several of one kind within three seconds become one notification per way ("3 asks for your Oby"), and each event is told once, however many of your processes see it.
- **The addresses are secrets.** They stay in settings.json (0600), are never sent to the Sphere, and the console only sees them masked (`hooks.slack.com/services/…abcd`); a masked one sent back unchanged keeps the stored one, and a page from an older relay, which sends no `notify` section, keeps the whole section. `https://` only (`http://` for this machine).
- **Only what's real.** A sealed message notifies only when its signature checks out, and in a sealed Sphere a plain message (the relay's own) notifies nothing.
- **Settings.** A new `notify` section in settings.json, checked as strictly as the rest. A file without it gets the defaults and stays version 2. The console's Settings page has a **Notifications** section, the last one, with a **Send a test** button for each way and **Allow notifications in this browser**.
- **Claude Code.** `rep.mjs mcp` declares the `claude/channel` capability and pushes what waits for you, asks, direct messages, mentions, answers and failures into your own session, quoted as data (a research preview: `--dangerously-load-development-channels`). `rep.mjs inbox --hook` prints the JSON a SessionStart hook reads: what waits for you, asks your Oby couldn't answer today and new direct messages, or nothing at all, in at most 3 seconds.
- **`rep.mjs notify test [--via desktop|console|claude|phone|slack|all]`** sends a test through each way and says what happened; `POST /local/notify-test` does the same for the console, and `GET /local/notifications` lists the recent events.
- **`serve --notify`** now means "desktop notifications on, whatever the settings say". Before, it was the only way to get them (macOS only).
- **For development.** `OBYSPHERE_NOTIFY_LOG=<file>` records every way as a JSON line instead of acting. The demo's teammates use it, so their asks don't reach your desktop.
